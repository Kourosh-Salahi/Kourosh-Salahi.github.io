An essay title goes here

A one-sentence summary of what the essay is about. This line also appears on the writing index.

Published Month 2026Reading time 5 minutes

Open with the thing you actually want to say. The first paragraph should earn the second one; a reader who stops here should still leave with the main idea.

Then build the case. Concrete beats abstract: a specific lab, a specific failure, a specific number. If a paragraph could appear in anyone's essay, cut it or make it yours.

A section heading, if the essay needs one

Short essays rarely need more than two or three sections. Use headings to mark a turn in the argument, not to decorate.

A pulled quote or a sentence worth setting apart.

Close by returning to the opening idea with whatever changed along the way. Resist the urge to summarize; say the one thing you now believe more strongly.
